bio
Raghav Chalapathy is a Staff Data Scientist at Walmart Global Tech. His work focuses on deep learning for anomaly detection, natural language processing, and time-series forecasting. He earned his PhD from the University of Sydney and has worked across industry and research at CSIRO's Data61, the NSW Ministry of Health, and Huawei Technologies. He was part of the Walmart Applied AI team that won both the Edelman and INFORMS awards in 2023. He holds CISSP and Cloudera Certified Data Scientist certifications, and is passionate about teaching AI — running the CausalAI Lab community and a Substack.
